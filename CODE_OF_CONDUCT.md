# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in AbstractNote to be a welcoming, harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and considerate in issues, pull requests, reviews and discussions.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully. Critique the work, not the person.
- Assume good faith, and prefer evidence over assertion: link to code, tests or measurements.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults or derogatory comments; personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without explicit
  permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or other conduct that would reasonably be considered
  inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (the repository, issues, pull requests and
discussions) and when someone officially represents the project in public spaces.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers at contact@abstractcore.ai. Reports
are handled confidentially, and we respond as promptly as circumstances allow. For security
vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of opening a public issue.

## Enforcement

Maintainers clarify these standards and may take any action they deem appropriate, including
editing or removing comments, commits, code and issues, or temporarily or permanently banning a
contributor for behaviour they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.
