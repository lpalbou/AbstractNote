  1. Strip: live dot (pulsing mint while live, amber paused, red broken) + one sentence
     ("Understanding your notes · 2 per minute · about 1 h 05 left"; "Paused · finishing the note in progress ·

     forbids a Pause/Resume verb swap), Repair (only when any helper is unhealthy), Reflect now.
  2. Right now: a card per active item; a drain is a dashed card with its notes nested (title opens the note,
     kind, Quick/Thinking pill, elapsed clock, stepper Reading · Extracting · Linking · Applying with the current
     step pulsing, sections meter); Cancel on a drain with an inline confirmation (no browser dialog).
     Consolidation card (lead addendum): stepper Normalize · Themes · Labels · Summaries · Then and now · Reflect,
     meter "Summaries · 72 of 140", elapsed + time left, last run line ("Yesterday: 17 entities joined, 35 themes,

     schedule picker (12h / 1d / 7d / off → PUT /note/settings {consolidate_schedule}); `consolidation: null` →
     "Consolidation is not available in your notebook yet." (no retry loop). Settings drawer shows the same picker.
  3. Pace: per-minute figures (5 min, 1 h), the 2-hour line (single series, 2 px, 10 % wash, ringed end dot,
     hairline grid, 0 / nice max ticks, crosshair + tooltip on hover and arrow keys, last still-filling 5 minutes
     dashed), queue by priority as a stacked bar (one hue in four ordered steps, 2 px gaps, 4 px ends, legend with
     words and counts) with the time left; waiting notes (GET /note/queue `waiting`, priority then oldest, first 50,
     title search, "Move to front" = POST /note/queue/{id}/priority {priority: 3}; urgent ones read "First in line").
  4. Just understood: newest first, new entries slide in (off under reduced motion), title, summary, chips
     "+3 entities · +9 claims · 1 replaced", duration, age; click opens the note.
  5. Needs attention: failed notes (error sentence, tries, age, Retry); helpers needing a repair with the reason;
     helper states (Understanding new notes / Looking back / Consolidation); reflect line (last look back, insights,
     themes / not named / changed since named) with Reflect now.
